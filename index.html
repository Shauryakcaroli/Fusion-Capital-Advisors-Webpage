§ 01 — The Firm

A boutique advisory practice built around the deal, not the org chart.

We advise on transactions that materially shape the trajectory of a business — sales of control, capital formation, cross-border combinations, and strategic realignments.

Fusion Capital Advisors is structured deliberately as a senior-led practice. Founders engage directly with founders and boards. Mandates are accepted selectively, executed quietly, and measured by outcome rather than activity.

Our coverage spans Indian mid-market corporates, growth-stage businesses, and family-owned enterprises evaluating succession, recapitalisation, or partnership with strategic and financial sponsors. We bring institutional rigour to the work — modelling, structuring, negotiation, diligence — without the layered handoffs that characterise larger houses.

Discretion is foundational. So is conviction in the position we take alongside our clients, all the way through close.

§ 06 — Why Fusion

A boutique by design.

i

Founder-led execution

Mandates are run by partners, not handed to junior teams. The senior banker who pitches the work is the senior banker who closes it.

ii

Senior attention, end-to-end

From origination through diligence and documentation, attention is undivided. Volume is deliberately constrained.

iii

Sector breadth, depth where it counts

Coverage across ten-plus sectors, with concentrated expertise in industrials, consumer, technology, and cross-border combinations.

iv

Strategic, not transactional

We treat each engagement as a position to be defended over years, not a single fee event. Our advice reflects that horizon.

v

Hands-on through close

Modelling, term-sheet negotiation, diligence orchestration, legal-documentation oversight — executed in-house, with conviction.

vi

Discretion as default

We work quietly. Most of our best work is never publicly attributed, and that is by intention.
